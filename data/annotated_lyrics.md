# Annotated Lyrics Corpus — Travis Scott's *UTOPIA*

## Overview

This file contains selected, short lyrical excerpts from Travis Scott's
*UTOPIA* (2023), annotated for cognitive-linguistic and multimodal analysis.

The annotations identify:

- Conceptual metaphors
- Image schemas
- Force-dynamic relations
- Spatial mappings
- Conceptual-blending inputs
- Multimodal interpretive functions

The lyrical annotations are analyzed in conjunction with the acoustic features
documented in the Multimodal Mapping Matrix (MMM).

> **Copyright and research-use note:** Only brief excerpts are included here
> for identification and scholarly analysis. The repository does not reproduce
> complete lyrics.

### Cognitive Interpretation

The lyrical structure of *HYAENA* establishes a conceptual environment in
which achievement is framed not as arrival at a stable destination but as
continuous movement through a competitive and potentially hostile environment.

The interaction between **PATH**, **FORCE**, and **WILDERNESS** provides the
conceptual basis for the proposed **Cyber-Tribal Blend**. The lyrical frame
combines primal survival imagery with contemporary technological and
commercial contexts.

5. Metaphor Identification Procedure

Linguistic expressions were examined using the Metaphor Identification
Procedure (MIP) developed by the Pragglejaz Group.

The procedure involved:

Identifying potentially metaphorical lexical units.
Establishing their contextual meanings.
Determining their more basic meanings where applicable.
Comparing contextual and basic meanings.
Marking expressions as metaphorically used when the contextual meaning
contrasts with and is understood through the more basic meaning.

The resulting metaphorical expressions were subsequently mapped onto
candidate image schemas and conceptual domains.

7. Analytical Status of the Annotations

The annotations represent interpretive analytical mappings, not claims
that individual words or acoustic frequencies automatically activate
particular cognitive schemas.

The proposed Multimodal Mapping Matrix (MMM) therefore treats acoustic and
linguistic features as context-sensitive cues whose cognitive
interpretation emerges from their interaction within a multimodal
configuration.

This distinction is important because the framework is intended as a
methodological tool for systematic multimodal analysis rather than as a
deterministic psychophysiological model.
